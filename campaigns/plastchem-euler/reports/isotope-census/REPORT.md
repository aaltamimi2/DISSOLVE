Parents were generated by clearing atom isotope flags, removing ordinary explicit hydrogen and cleaning stereo assignments. Two CHNO parents, perylene and dibutyl phthalate, already have identity-verified pilot results. The DEHP parent completed DFT but failed the current strict stereo-identity check; a mapping decision would not resolve or override that separate acceptance failure. The other six CHNO parents were outside the pilot. No result has been reused or attributed to an isotopologue in this audit.

## Options for the owner — neither selected

**Option A: exclude isotope-labelled entries from the campaign scope as analytical standards.** If the owner adopts this scope rule, retain all source records and mark the labelled keys explicitly excluded, with the policy and reason. Isotope labelling identifies these entries; it does not by itself prove every entry’s use classification.

| Tier | Labelled entries excluded | Remaining unique targets |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| CHNO | 9 | 5,824 |
| heteroatoms | 1 | 1,720 |
| Si_B | 0 | 238 |
| All tiers | 10 | 7,782 |

For CHNO, this is **5,824 future unique targets plus 9 explicitly excluded source identities**, not a silent reduction of the pinned 5,833-key set. The original files, row mappings and completed pilot denominator remain intact.

**Option B: calculate each unlabelled electronic parent once and retain explicit isotopologue-to-parent attribution.** Under the Born–Oppenheimer electronic model described by the orchestrator, the isotopologue and parent share the equilibrium electronic geometry and COSMO sigma surface; their entry identities differ. This is reuse of an electronic surface, not a direct isotope-specific calculation, and does not claim equality of isotope-dependent vibrational or nuclear-motion properties. Independently run numerical optimisations are still subject to convergence and CPU differences.

For CHNO, this keeps **5,833 source identities backed by 5,824 distinct electronic targets**, because all nine parents already exist. For the parked heteroatom tier, **1,721 source identities still require 1,721 electronic targets**: the isotope-labelled input is replaced at the calculation layer by one otherwise absent parent. These are target counts, not new authorised job counts; completed acceptable parent results would only be reused under an approved provenance policy.

A future mapping result should retain `source_inchikey`, `calculation_parent_inchikey`, isotope composition, mapping-policy/version and owner authority, parent input/deck digests, parent surface digest and CPU model, and the actual post-optimisation **parent** InChIKey. It must explicitly identify a mapped electronic result and link the original source rows. Never relabel a parent’s geometry-derived key as the isotope-labelled key, and do not bypass parent convergence or identity validation. The submitted XYZ represents the unlabelled parent.

Neither option has been applied. All tiers retain their pinned counts, task 42 remains a reported preflight failure with denominator 56, and Phase 2 remains unauthorised.
